# Golf Geek
- Take a picture of your scorecard post round and save it to your profile
- Enter in hole by hole FIR, GIR, # of putts and Score to track % over the season
- Handicap calculation based on rounds inputed into your profile

This app calculates an unofficial handicap index for personal tracking. PCC (Playing Conditions Calculation) is assumed to be 0, as official PCC data requires access to the USGA/R&A's centralized scoring pool, which isn't available to third-party apps.


Score Differential = 113/Slope Rating X (adjusted gross score - course rating - PCC)
